Download for Mac Already installed? Open Backlot

For Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.

Already using Backlot? Update through Backlot → Check for Updates first. The Open Backlot button opens team setup in build 364 or later.

  1. Install. Open the downloaded DMG and drag Backlot into Applications. Then open it from Applications.
  2. Sign in with Google. Use your @movieguide.org work account. Personal Gmail accounts do not open the Movieguide workspace.
  3. Meet your team. Choose your superior and direct reports in setup. Existing users can open Home → Set up your team / requests.
  4. Confirm your requests. A reporting relationship becomes active only after the other person confirms. Direct managers can then add, edit and complete their reports’ Home tasks. Reporting lines do not share private pages.
  5. Try Learn Backlot. Your practice page includes checklists, callouts, a database and a full Word-style document.

This internal release is self-signed and not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks the first launch, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway after trying to open the app. Full installation guide.
